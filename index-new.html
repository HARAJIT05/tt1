FAQ

Common Questions

How long is each physiotherapy session?

A typical session lasts 30 to 45 minutes, depending on your condition and treatment plan. The first visit takes a little longer as it includes a full assessment.

Do I need a doctor's referral to book a session?

No referral is required. You can book a physiotherapy assessment directly. If your condition needs medical investigation, you will be advised and the physiotherapist will coordinate with your doctor where needed.

Is home physiotherapy available?

Yes. Home physiotherapy is available across Gouranga Nagar and greater Kolkata for patients who find it difficult to travel, including elderly patients and those recovering from surgery or injury.

What conditions do you treat?

Treatment is offered for back and neck pain, sciatica, sports injuries, knee and joint pain, frozen shoulder, arthritis, post-surgery rehabilitation, neurological conditions such as stroke and paralysis, and geriatric care for older adults.

Is physiotherapy painful?

Physiotherapy should not be painful. Some techniques may cause mild, temporary discomfort, but treatment is always adjusted to your comfort level. The goal is to relieve pain, not add to it.
