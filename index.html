Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions patients in Kolkata ask most often about physiotherapy.

How many physiotherapy sessions do I need for back pain?

It varies with the cause and severity, but most patients with back pain see noticeable improvement within 6–8 sessions. Acute issues may need fewer, while chronic or post-surgery cases may need a longer, structured plan. After your first assessment I'll give you a clear, personalised estimate.

Do you provide physiotherapy at home?

Yes. I offer home physiotherapy across Gouranga Nagar and greater Kolkata for patients who find it difficult to travel — including elderly patients and those recovering from surgery or injury. The full session is carried out at your doorstep.

Do I need a doctor's referral to book a session?

No referral is required. You can book a physiotherapy assessment directly. If your condition needs medical investigation, I'll advise you and coordinate with your doctor where needed.

What conditions do you treat?

I treat back & neck pain, sciatica, sports injuries, knee and joint pain, frozen shoulder, arthritis, post-surgery rehabilitation, neurological conditions (such as stroke and paralysis), and provide geriatric care for older adults.

How long is each physiotherapy session?

A typical session lasts 30–45 minutes, depending on your condition and the treatment plan. Your first visit takes a little longer as it includes a full assessment.

Is physiotherapy painful?

Physiotherapy should not be painful. Some techniques may cause mild, temporary discomfort, but treatment is always adjusted to your comfort level. The goal is to relieve pain, not add to it.
