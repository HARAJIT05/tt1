About Me

Compassionate care, built around your recovery

I'm a professional physiotherapist based in Gouranga Nagar, Kolkata, committed to helping people of all ages overcome pain, restore movement, and improve their quality of life. Every treatment plan I create is tailored to your body, your goals, and your daily routine.

From acute injuries to long-term conditions, I use modern, evidence-based physiotherapy techniques alongside hands-on therapy and guided exercise to deliver real, lasting results.

  • 🎓 Qualified Physiotherapist (BPT)
  • 🏥 Clinic-based & home-visit treatments
  • 🤝 Patient-first, one-on-one sessions
  • 📍 Serving Gouranga Nagar & greater Kolkata
